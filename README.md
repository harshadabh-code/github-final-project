# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple program that calculates simple interest on a principal amount.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100

- **P** = Principal amount
- **R** = Annual rate of interest (%)
- **T** = Time period (in years)

## Example
Principal = 1000, Rate = 5%, Time = 2 years
Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = **100**

## How to Use
1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the rate of interest.
3. Enter the time in years.
4. The program displays the simple interest and the total amount.
